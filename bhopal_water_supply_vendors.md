## 2. Cluster & Regional Breakdown in Bhopal

### A. Kolar Road Cluster
* **Coverage:** Danish Kunj, Rajved Colony, Salaiya, Chunabhatti, Kolar Police Station circle.
* **Vendors Active:** Sharma Water Supplier, Bhumika Water Tanker Supplier, Rohit Meena Water Supplier, 1 St Dew Drinking Water, Pyour Water Suppliers, SS Enterprises.
* **Supply Characteristics:** High reliance on both 20L jar subscriptions (hard groundwater area) and 5,000L–10,000L tankers during peak summer months.

### B. Arera Colony, Shahpura & Gulmohar Cluster
* **Coverage:** Arera Colony (E-1 to E-8, 10 No. Market, 11 No. Stop), Trilanga, Shahpura, Gulmohar Colony.
* **Vendors Active:** Kesari Enterprises, Baba Shankar Jal Sewa, Oracle Valley Drinking Water (Abha Enterprises), PaniWala.
* **Supply Characteristics:** Dense residential societies and commercial offices; predominantly 20L premium chilled/RO jars with scheduled morning delivery.

### C. Govindpura, Indrapuri & Ayodhya Nagar Cluster
* **Coverage:** Govindpura Industrial Estate, Kailash Nagar, C-Sector Indrapuri, Ayodhya Nagar Phase-1/2, Narela Shankari.
* **Vendors Active:** M S Water Supplier, Balaji Beverages, Woow Beverages, Guru Kripa Water Supplier, SR Enterprise Water Supplier, Aarohi Food & Beverages.
* **Supply Characteristics:** Combination of industrial bulk bottling, commercial packaging, and residential 20L jar delivery.

### D. MP Nagar, TT Nagar & Central Commercial Hub
* **Coverage:** Zone-1 & Zone-2 MP Nagar, DB Mall vicinity, Mata Mandir, New Market, TT Nagar.
* **Vendors Active:** Kings Ice Cube & Water Supplier, Jagdamba Water Supplier, Narmada Agency.
* **Supply Characteristics:** Heavy B2B and institutional demand (offices, coaching institutes, food courts, restaurants).

### E. Katara Hills, Misrod & Saket Nagar / AIIMS Cluster
* **Coverage:** Katara Hills, Spring Valley, Bagli, Narmadapuram Road, Misrod, Indus Town, Saket Nagar (AIIMS hospital zone).
* **Vendors Active:** Maa Narmade Neer RO Water Supply, Ezeeway Enterprises, Mukesh Water Tanker Supplier, Ramsingh Water Tanker Supplier.
* **Supply Characteristics:** Large newly developed multi-story townships (Spring Valley, Indus Town, Sagar Golden Palm) requiring both bulk tankers and daily 20L cans.

### F. Karond, Berasia Road & Old City
* **Coverage:** Karond Bypass Road, Maurya Colony, Nariyal Kheda, Balvihar Road.
* **Vendors Active:** Ravi Jal Sewa, Shri Balaji Chilled Water.
* **Supply Characteristics:** Chilled water cans, event water supplies, and tanker support for unpiped areas.
